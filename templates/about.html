About M MUSICS

M MUSICS is a marketplace that connects people with professional musicians, composers, producers, trainers and quality instruments, starting in Uganda.

Our mission

To make professional musical services, music education and quality instruments more accessible through a reliable, convenient and professionally managed platform.

Our vision

To become a leading music services and equipment marketplace that connects musical talent, creativity, education and technology.

How it works

Customers find a musician, composer, producer or trainer, send a request, agree a quote, pay and review the work. They can also buy instruments and accessories from the shop.

Providers create a profile with a photo and audio sample, set their rates, and manage booking requests. Profiles go public after approval, and verified providers get a badge.
